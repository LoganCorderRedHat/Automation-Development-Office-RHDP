This is where you decide what ADO builds on your cluster.

== Automation Profile

Leave *Profile* on *Custom — Choose components manually*.

Profiles such as *Virtualization* or *Full Platform* select a set of related components for you. In this workshop you pick them yourself.

== Components

. Click *All Components*. The catalog is in alphabetical order. The *Applications*, *OpenShift Options*, and *Operators* buttons filter it, and the search box finds a component by name.
. Check the five components below, in the order they appear in the catalog. When you check one, its settings open under it.

=== ACM Operator

Installs Red Hat Advanced Cluster Management for Kubernetes and creates its MultiClusterHub in namespace `open-cluster-management`.

Leave the defaults:

* *Operator Channel*: `release-2.17`
* *Enable Multicluster Observability*: unchecked (it needs S3 object storage, which this lab does not have)
* *Generate namespace-label policy*: unchecked

=== Admin HTPasswd

Adds an HTPasswd login to your cluster with a cluster-admin user. ADO adds it next to your current login; it does not remove anything.

[cols="1,2"]
|===
|Field |What to enter

|User change (IdP stays)
|Leave *add (merge users)*

|Login button name (IdP)
|Leave `{htpasswd_idp}`

|Username
|`{htpasswd_user}` (replace `admin`)

|Password
|A password of your choice. Write it down: you log in with it at the end.

|Role
|Leave `cluster-admin`
|===

Why `{htpasswd_user}`: you already log in as `{openshift_cluster_admin_username}`. A different name makes the new login easy to spot.

=== Console Banner

Adds a banner to the top of the OpenShift console.

* *Console Banner Text*: replace the default with a message of your own, for example `Built with ADO!`
* *Console Banner Background Color* and *Console Banner Text Color*: pick any colors you like.
* Leave *Banner action* on *Add (keep existing ADO banners)* and *Console Banner Location* on *BannerTop*.

=== Migration Toolkit for Virtualization

Installs the Migration Toolkit for Virtualization (MTV) and creates its ForkliftController in namespace `openshift-mtv`.

Leave the defaults: *Operator Channel* `release-v2.12` and *Namespace* `openshift-mtv`.

=== OCP Virtualization

Installs OpenShift Virtualization and creates its HyperConverged resource in namespace `openshift-cnv`.

Leave the defaults:

* *Operator channel*: `stable`
* *Enable KubeSecondaryDNS*: unchecked
* *Also install Migration Toolkit for Virtualization (MTV)*: checked (you selected MTV above)
* *Enable Multicluster Observability (Fleet Virt metrics)*: unchecked

== Check your selection

. Click *Selected*. You see your five components, and the header shows *5 selected*.
. *ACM Operator*, *Admin HTPasswd*, and *Console Banner* show *Ready*. If *Admin HTPasswd* shows *Attention Needed*, its password is still empty.
. *Migration Toolkit for Virtualization* and *OCP Virtualization* show *Not validated*. That is expected: Preflight does not have field checks for them yet, and Bootstrap still works.

Click *Next*.
